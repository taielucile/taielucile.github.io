Hello,I’m Taiel

I’m an undergraduate sociologist at UCF. I study how institutions shape inequality and opportunity across people’s lives, with a focus on race, disability, and socioeconomic disadvantage.

My current projects examine disability classification, developmental-service timing, and education policy. I’m interested in how organizational practices and professional judgments affect access to support—and how people navigate and change those institutions.

From Kingston to Orlando

I was born in Kingston and came to UCF through community college, after working at McDonald’s, in call centers, and in healthcare. I started college planning to become a speech-language pathologist. The questions I kept asking about race, language, and special education led me to sociology.

I live in Florida with my wife and three funky cats—Diamond, Willow, and Ru.
