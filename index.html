Hello,I’m Taiel

I’m an undergraduate sociologist at UCF. My work asks how schools and public institutions shape the lives of Black and disabled young people.

I work with school records and national surveys to study disability classification and the timing of developmental services.

From Kingston to Orlando

I was born in Jamaica and grew up between Jamaica and the United States. I’m now studying sociology at UCF, with minors in Cultural Anthropology and Africana Studies. Before sociology, I planned to become a school-based speech-language pathologist.

At home, I have three cats—Diamond Celine, Willow Bean, and RuPaul Charles. Diamond is the oldest at 12.
